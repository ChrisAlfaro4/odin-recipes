Pizza Recipe

Description

Pizza is one of the most popular foods in the world. It is made with a round dough base, tomato sauce, cheese, and different toppings. Some popular toppings include pepperoni, mushrooms, olives, and vegetables. Pizza can be enjoyed for lunch, dinner, or as a snack.

There are many different types of pizza, such as cheese pizza, pepperoni pizza, and vegetarian pizza. Each type has its own unique flavor and ingredients. Pizza is often shared with family and friends, making it a great choice for parties and special occasions.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound pizza dough
  • 1/2 cup tomato sauce
  • 1/4 cup grated mozzarella cheese
  • 1/4 cup sliced pepperoni
  • 1/4 cup sliced mushrooms
  • 1/4 cup sliced olives
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  1. Preheat the oven to 475°F (245°C).
  2. Roll out the pizza dough on a floured surface.
  3. Spread the tomato sauce over the dough.
  4. Sprinkle the mozzarella cheese over the sauce.
  5. Add the desired toppings.
  6. Bake for 12-15 minutes or until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbly.
