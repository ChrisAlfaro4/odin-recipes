Pasta Recipe

Description

This delicious garlic butter pasta is a simple and flavorful recipe perfect for a quick meal. Start by boiling your favorite pasta in salted water until tender. While the pasta cooks, melt butter in a pan, add minced garlic, and sauté until fragrant. Drain the pasta and toss it into the pan with the garlic butter, mixing well to coat every strand.

For extra flavor, sprinkle freshly grated Parmesan cheese, black pepper, and chopped parsley over the pasta. You can also add a squeeze of lemon juice for a fresh, zesty taste. Serve warm and enjoy a comforting homemade meal that is easy to prepare and perfect for any occasion.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound pasta
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/4 cup chopped parsley
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  1. Boil the pasta in salted water until tender, then drain.
  2. In a large pan, melt the butter over medium heat and sauté the minced garlic until fragrant.
  3. Add the drained pasta to the pan and toss to coat with the garlic butter.
  4. Sprinkle with grated Parmesan cheese, black pepper, and chopped parsley. Add a squeeze of lemon juice if desired.
  5. Serve warm and enjoy!
