Burger Recipe

Description

A classic burger is a simple yet satisfying meal. It consists of a juicy beef patty served on a toasted bun, topped with lettuce, tomato, onion, and your choice of condiments. You can customize it with various cheeses, sauces, and additional toppings to suit your taste.

There are many variations of burgers, from the traditional cheeseburger to gourmet options with unique ingredients and flavors. Burgers are perfect for a quick lunch or a casual dinner, and they can be enjoyed at home or at your favorite fast-food restaurant.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound beef patties
  • 4 hamburger buns
  • 4 slices cheese
  • 4 leaves lettuce
  • 4 slices tomato
  • 4 slices onion
  • 1 tablespoon ketchup
  1. Preheat the grill or a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. Season the beef patties with salt and pepper.
  3. Grill or cook the patties for 3-4 minutes on each side, or until desired doneness is reached.
  4. Toast the hamburger buns on the grill or in a toaster.
  5. Assemble the burgers by placing a patty on the bottom bun, followed by the desired toppings.
  6. Top with the remaining bun and serve immediately.
